# cfu_curate.py — the CFU curation window (v29, reworked Oct 5 2026; tuning overlay Oct 10; multi-select + "looks" Oct 11)

User request (Oct 5): zoom into the CFU map; a movie ("video session") panel alongside that can be stopped and started freely, to judge the size of a CFU while curating; overlapping CFUs in different colours because they were hard to tell apart and to select. Oct 11 (on NavRearChrTIGRE-2608-2 261001_004): drag-select several CFUs at once because they are densely packed; the pipeline should ideally distinguish non-afferent structures (bright compact blobs) itself.

## What the window does now

- **Left column:** the runs (kept/total per run) and, below, the **CFUs of the current run** (number, events, **looks**, kept / noise / added), each row in the CFU's colour. Clicking a row selects the CFU — the easy way to pick one of several overlapping CFUs; ctrl/shift-click selects several (Treeview `selectmode="extended"`). `Tab` / `Shift-Tab` (also `.` / `,` and the `< CFU` / `CFU >` buttons) step through them.
- **Top left — map:** mean image (AQuA2's datPro) with every CFU outlined. Colours come from a greedy graph colouring on a 19-colour palette: CFUs that overlap or come within 3 px of each other never share a colour (`assign_colours`; bounding boxes prune the pair checks). Noise = thin grey; hand-drawn = cyan; selected = white over black, number label bold. Clicking where several CFUs overlap cycles through them. Outlines are `skimage.measure.find_contours` paths in one `LineCollection` per panel (one `ax.contour` per CFU was ~10× slower with 106 CFUs).
- **Top right — movie:** the binned tiff (`tifffile.memmap`, page-by-page fallback) cropped to the CFU maps' frame, with the same outlines. Controls: play/pause (`space`), `←/→` one frame (`Shift`: 10), `Home/End`, a full-width frame slider, fps box (2–30), `raw` / `dF/F` view (`v`; dF/F = (F − F0)/F0 per pixel, F0 = median of ~60 frames spread over the run, computed on first use), contrast slider, "outlines on movie" checkbox, `[` / `]` jump to the previous / next event of the selected CFU (3 frames before the onset; the frame text names the event), "whole field (0)". Clicking in the trace or the raster moves the movie to that time. Playback and frame steps use blitting (animated artists: the movie image, its outline collections, the frame text, the time cursors in trace and raster; background re-captured on every full draw via the `draw_event`), ~50 ms per frame with 106 CFUs; a full redraw (selection change) ~0.45 s. Playback keeps running across a redraw.
- **Zoom / pan:** scroll wheel over either image zooms around the cursor (`+`/`-` keys too), both panels share their axes; right- or middle-drag pans; `0` resets. The view is kept across CFU selections and across runs of the same image size.
- **Middle:** the selected CFU's dF/F curve with onset markers in its colour and the blue time cursor; numbers + key help on the right (now with the CFU's "looks" reason). **Bottom:** onset raster (one `scatter` call), selected CFU's row highlighted, time cursor.
- **Crop alignment:** AQuA2 registers and crops the movie (regMaskGap + motion margin, not symmetric — e.g. tiff 512×796 vs maps 499×781). `Movie.align_to()` finds where datPro sits in the raw frame by normalised cross-correlation (`skimage.feature.match_template` of datPro against the mean of 10 sampled raw frames) and uses that (row, column) offset for the movie crop and for hand-drawn regions (`crop_gap` is now `(gy, gx)`; `split_gap()` accepts the old int). Falls back to the symmetric estimate when the match is < 0.5. Verified on a synthetic movie with a known (6, 6) offset (match 0.99).
- **Keyboard:** one root `<Key>` binding (the earlier mpl `key_press_event` + root binding fired twice); ignored while an Entry/Combobox/Scale has focus; buttons never take focus; `<ISO_Left_Tab>` bound in a try (not a keysym on Windows).
- Decisions, sidecar format, propagation and the CLI are unchanged; `--fps` sets the playback speed; `--um-per-px` (default 0.792; the GUI passes the top-row value) feeds the fibre mask.

## Several CFUs at once (Oct 11 2026)
`self.multi` (set of CFU indices) next to `self.sel` (the CFU whose trace is shown). Left-button press on either image records `_drag`; a move of > 4 px draws a dashed cyan rubber band (`Rectangle` patches on both panels, blitted with the animated artists); release → `_box_select`: every CFU whose centroid lies in the box or ≥ 50 % of whose footprint does; shift- or ctrl-drag adds to the selection; a release without movement is the old click (cycle through overlapping CFUs), ctrl-click toggles one CFU in the multi-selection; Esc clears; the list's selection mirrors `multi` (`sync_ctree` / `on_ctree` both ways). `mark()` applies to `multi ∪ {sel}`; the selected ones are drawn thick white-over-black and their labels bold; the title says "N selected (k / x act on all)". Help text updated.

## "looks" — a first opinion per CFU (Oct 11 2026, `afferent2p/cfu_shape.py`)
`cfu_features(masks, mean_img, um_per_px)` per CFU: area, elongation (sqrt of the principal-moment ratio), `on_fibre` (share of the footprint on `tune.injection_mask` of the registered mean image — `_AQuA2_meanimg.tif`, same crop frame as the maps, else datPro — dilated 2 px), `bright_ratio` (mean image inside / median of a 2–7 px ring, above the 1st percentile). Rules: `on_fibre < 0.25` → **off-fibre**; compact (elongation < 1.8) and bright (ratio > 1.6) and `on_fibre < 0.5` → **blob**; else **fibre**. Shown as "fibre 85% / off-fibre 5% / blob 12%" in the list (non-fibre rows tinted), in the text panel with the reason, in the title ("N look non-fibre (u selects them)"); **Select suggested (u)** puts them in the multi-selection; **f** overlays the fibre mask in orange. `--list --suggest` prints them per run. It is explicitly a suggestion (a bright varicosity on a fibre stays fibre; a faint fibre the mask misses is flagged wrongly). On 2608-1 (mirror): 260921_004 → 21 of 39 CFUs non-fibre, forming exactly the vertical vessel-wall band next to the vessel crossing (the sweep's hotspot) plus a few blobs in the dark region; 260921_006 → ~18 of 35 the same band; 260921_002 → CFU 1 blob; other runs 0–1 flagged. This bears on the max-power FOV2 result (51 → 144 events): a large share of its CFUs are on the vessel wall; after curation the effect must be recomputed (tab 6 + tab 7). Not done: using the suggestion automatically in the analysis (`event_source` option) — offered once the user has judged the rule on 2608-2.

## Tuning overlay (Oct 10 2026)
Why: after the first tuning sweep the user wanted to look at the events a candidate setting (thr 1.5) found, but the sweep leaves only event tables, no CFU files — the window said "has no *_res_cfu.mat yet" when he pointed it at the sweep. Now `python cfu_curate.py "<mouse>" --tune N [--tune-variant real|null|inject]` (GUI tab 5 field "overlay the events of tuning setting #", → `--tune N`) reads `aqua_tune\<run>\sNN\<variant>\*_AQuA2_events.csv` (`tune_events_records`) and adds every event as a read-only added-style record `{"source": "tune", label "T<k> sNN (<setting label from tune_manifest.json>)", polygon = the event's box (1-based csv → 0-based ±0.5), onset_frames [t0], t1_frame, peak_dff, note}`: magenta (`TUNE_COLOUR`) boxes labelled T1… on both panels, rows "T<k> tuning | fr <t0> | dF/F x" in the CFU list, trace = mean of the box read from the movie in ONE pass over all boxes (`Movie.frame(t, "raw")`, then `trace_to_events` for dF/F), `[` `]` jump to the onset, the trace/raster cursor work. `save()` writes only non-tune added records; a run with no CFU file but a tuning result opens on the sweep's exported mean image (`tune_only` stub data, n = 0 CFUs) with the boxes alone; a run without tuning results logs a note. Title: "N tuning events (sNN) in magenta". Boxes are in AQuA2's crop frame of the sweep run, which equals the batch's crop (same registration). Tested under Xvfb on the test folder (106 CFUs + 32 simulated tuning events; tune-only run; save writes 0 added records). Setting numbers = the "setting" column of `tune_recommendation.txt` (0 = current).
