## The measurement in two numbers (how v39 presents it)
CATCH — 72 fake transients are planted on the fibres (3 brightnesses × 3 sizes × short/long); catch = the share the setting finds. NOISE — in a copy of the movie with every pixel's time course scrambled (no real events left) everything found is invented; noise = those events as a share of what the setting reports in the real movie. The pick = the highest dot left of the red line (noise ≤ 10 %, ≥ 80 % of its events on the fibres). `<mouse>_tune.png` (v39) = one panel per day × FOV, pre and post together, every setting a dot at (noise, catch) labelled "thr/minSize", sized by the events/min it reports on the fibres, the rule's pick starred, the pair the day uses ringed green, the batch's setting in a red dotted ring; plain sentences under each panel (the same open `tune_recommendation.txt` as IN PLAIN WORDS). The curves are `<mouse>_tune_detail.png` (v38: amplitude classes as shades + distinct markers — in the v37 file the three dF/F lines were all black with identical legend entries, a bug).

## Readings of the Oct 25 sweep
- Everywhere catch grows towards thr 1.5 / minSize 20; the dim class (0.3) is found 0–0.2 (0.36–0.46 only on the quiet FOV1 movies at thr 1.5), 0.6 → 0.5–0.9, 1.0 → 0.7–1.0: at any clean setting the detector catches transients reported ≳ 0.3 dF/F and misses weaker ones.
- thr 1.5 / 20 is dirty on most movies (noise 0.5–1.0: the scrambled movie yields as many events as the real one); thr 2 / 20 is 5–35 %; thr ≥ 2.5 or minSize ≥ 50 is clean but finds little.
- The user's reading "thr 2.0 seems best for 261006_001" (catch 62 %, 1.46 ev/min — the top of the recall and rate curves): right on sensitivity, but at thr 2 / 20 the scrambled movie yields 8 events against 23 real → 35 % noise (limit 10 %); the rule took thr 2.5 / 20 (11 events, 1 noise, catch 39 %). ~15 true events vs ~10, at the price of 1 in 3 being noise. The knob is `max false-alarm share` (tab 3): 0.35 accepts it; the analysis subtracts the expected noise from the rates (v36) but not from the CFU maps. v39 writes this trade-off in plain words under each panel ("More sensitive but rejected: … to take it anyway: max false-alarm share 0.35") and the picker lets the user choose by eye.
- 261006 (sham) FOV1: quiet (≤ 0.7 ev/min at any clean setting; matched thr 2/50 → 0.13 vs 0.06 ev/min). FOV2: v37 said TOO WEAK (0.24) because the pre's thr 2/20 had 11 % noise (4 null among 36 real) and 61 % on fibres; with v38 (grace + on-fibre relaxation + same-setting preference) thr 2/20 for both → 2.29 vs 2.29 ev/min — no change on the sham. (Before the same-setting preference the matched pair was pre thr 1.5/50 vs post thr 2/20 at catch 0.56/0.55 → 1.08 vs 2.29 ev/min: a 2× "increase" on a sham day — the strongest argument for preferring one setting.)
- 261014 FOV1: pre nearly silent (0.13 ev/min clean), post dirty at every permissive setting (1–2 null vs 1–2 real events) → NOT COMPARABLE at any rule; essentially no detectable afferent activity in FOV1 that day.
- 261014 FOV2 (the user's FOV): pre 002 finds nothing at any clean setting except thr 2/20 (4 events, 1 null → 25 %, 80 % on fibres; thr 1.5/20 = junk: 6 ev/min off fibres, 100 % noise); post 006 at thr 2/20: 1.40 ev/min on fibres, 96 % on, 5 % noise, catch 0.56 (chosen). v38 matches both at thr 2/20 (catch 0.60 / 0.56): 0.25 → 1.40 ev/min (4 → 22 events), fp 0.06/min both. The hand result on 008 (thr 2 / minSize 20 = the same setting) has 9.2 ev/min — 6× the sweep's 006 at the same setting: a later run (008 ≈ 30 min after 006) or a difference between the GUI run and the pipeline's; adding 008 to the plan + exporting the hand result settles it.
