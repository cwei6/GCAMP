## What is actually registered, by whom (code facts)

* The AQuA route (Yu's pipeline, reproduced by `unpack_bin.py`) does **no registration before AQuA2**: `.sbx` → mean of 31 raw frames (1.984 s) → `_bin31f_1p98s.tif` (global brightness flattening + flagged frames) → AQuA2. The lab's MATLAB registration belongs to the old projection/suite2p route and is not used here. The old afferent2p suite2p route (`afferent2p_out/<exp>/suite2p/plane0/data.bin`, rigid + nonrigid, aligned on the red channel) exists for 2608-2 day 260915 only.
* **The legacy MATLAB chain, read from the real code (Oct 15)** — `ProcessScanboxData_concatFirst.m` (spreadsheet `ImagingDatasets_Chao.xlsx`, dataSet `Afferents`, `regParam.method = 'rigid'` overriding the default affine/TurboReg; `DefaultProcessingParams`: edges `[80 80 40 40]`, binXY 2, no gamma / lowpass / highpass / median / temporal averaging / histmatch, chunkSize 100): all runs of a day are concatenated (`ConcatenateRuns` → `.sbxcat`), the reference epoch is the longest zero-speed epoch ≥ 30 s with 5 s trimmed off each end (`DetermineReference` → `BinStillEpochs 'speed'`, thresholds relaxed 1 s at a time if none), then **stage 1 `CorrectExpt`**: chunks of 15 scans, edges cropped, `imresize` ½ (bicubic), reference = mean of the chunk, central 95 % of the frame, `imgaussfilt` σ 1 on both, `dftregistrationAlex` (Guizar-Sicairos, usfac 100 → 1/100 px) → shifts × 2, no inter-chunk alignment for 2-D data (the 3-D branch has one), global median subtracted, applied to both channels with `imtranslate` (bilinear) → `.sbxdft` — it removes the within-second jitter but leaves the chunk-to-chunk drift; **stage 2 `AlignPlanes` → `RegisterSBX`**: reference = `uint16(mean)` of the reference scans of the `.sbxdft` movie, edges cropped, `binXY` (2 × 2 block mean), then per frame MATLAB's intensity-based `imregtform(frame, ref, 'rigid', RegularStepGradientDescent{MaximumIterations 1000, MinimumStepLength 1e-6, MaximumStepLength 0.001, RelaxationFactor 0.75}, MeanSquares)` (rotation + translation, parfor over frames), translation × 2, applied to the full frames with `imwarp(…, 'OutputView', imref2d(sz))` → `.sbxreg` (TurboReg/Fiji is only used when method = 'affine'); `GetDeformCat3D` only extracts/flags the transforms (shift limit ± 3.5 px); **stage 3 `GenerateExptProjections` → `WriteSbxPlaneTif`**: per run, bin = round(scanRate / rate_target) = 15 scans (1 Hz) starting at scan bin + 1 (the first second of every run is dropped), mean per bin → uint16, then the projection edges `[80 80 40 40]` cropped, written as `Projections\Runs\<expt>_run<i>_<type>_Z01_green.tif` for cat / dft / reg. The rigid stage is a local gradient-descent optimiser starting from the identity with a 0.001 maximum step: on the real data it moved the frames by 0.02–0.05 px (median) — see Result. The lab's loader (`LoadSBXinfo`) does its arithmetic in the class of the stored fields; the user's current info `.mat` files (re-saved from Python, Scanbox 3 format: `channels = -1`, `chan.nchan = 2`, `sz` / `recordsPerBuffer` / `resfreq` uint16) would saturate it — the wrapper below rebuilds the info in double precision.
* AQuA2 (public repo yu-lab-vt/AQuA2, commit of Oct 9 2026; the user's copy matches it — same `aqua_cmd_batch` error text and batch.csv columns; `AQuA2-main` also sits in `lagacy_matlab\Matlab`): with `registrateCorrect = 2`, `reg.regCrossCorrelation` builds the reference = mean of the **first 10 frames** (median-subtracted), finds for every frame the **integer** translation maximising the zero-padded FFT cross-correlation (`reg.calCC`), moves the frame, then crops `max/min` of the shifts off the borders. Before that `burst.prep1` crops `regMaskGap` (5 px) and scales to 0–1; after it `pre.bleach_correct` divides by a fitted monotone exponential of the whole-field mean. `res.datOrg1` in the saved `_AQuA2.mat` (`-v7.3`, uncompressed) is this registered + cropped + bleach-corrected movie, rescaled back to uint16 intensity — the frames the events, CFUs and event boxes refer to. The shifts themselves are not saved. AQuA2 reads tiffs with `io.readTiffSeq` (MATLAB `Tiff` class; plain multi-page uncompressed tiffs are fine).
* So: the detection does NOT run on the raw tiff; it runs on a rigidly steadied movie. What AQuA2's step cannot undo: motion within a 2-s bin (blur), non-rigid deformation, z-motion, sub-pixel residuals. The curation window, the report montages and the v32 heatmap traces used the RAW tiff (plus the crop offset found by template match) — that is why the movement was so visible there. Fixed in v33 (below).

## Measurements on 2608-2 day 260915 (both FOVs)

* Full-rate rigid shifts from the old suite2p registration (red channel, 15.6 Hz): median |shift| 1.4–3.2 px per raw frame, 95th pct 3–8 px, max 7–41 px; shifts grow through the session (jumps ≥ 2 px between raw frames: 4–16/min in the first runs, 115–200/min in the last). This mouse barely runs on 260915 (0.2–6 % of frames) — the movement is grooming/fidgeting/drift, not locomotion; corr(|shift|, speed) 0.04–0.40.
* Binned frames vs AQuA2's reference (our phase correlation, `afferent2p/motion.py`): 001 median 1.9 px (max 5.7), 002 median 2.8 px (max 7.9); jumps ≥ 2 px between consecutive binned frames 0.9/min (001) and 3.1/min (002); peak correlation 0.72–0.82 → the rigid registration of binned frames works well.
* Within-bin blur, measured on the 200-frame raw samples (`qc_raw/*_raw_sample.tif`, 12.8 s): per-bin shift range 1–5 px, but registering the raw frames before averaging changes the binned frame's contrast by only −1 % and its correlation to a sharp reference from 0.83 to 0.86 → for still periods the blur is negligible. (Per-bin ranges from the suite2p shifts were larger in the later runs; those are partly estimation noise on the red channel.)
* Residual after an AQuA2-style registration (emulated in numpy, crop reproduced to within 1 px of AQuA2's): rigid residual ≤ 0.6 px; **non-rigid deformation** (3 × 4 blocks) median 1.7 px in 001 (FOV 1), 0.7 px in 002 (FOV 2), growing slowly over the 16 min (a warp, not a flicker). Noise floor of the index with white noise at the real level (per-pixel SD ≈ 1000 at mean 2500!) is 0.2 px; a 0.5 % stretch reads 1.1 px. Deformation jumps (blocks moving ≥ ~2 px since the previous frame) are rare: 0.4/min.
* Events vs motion in 001 (14 events): 1 onset on a moving frame, 1.4 expected by chance → no sign that the detections follow the movement in this run.
* The tuning sweep's null movie (per-pixel circular time shift) destroys spatially coherent motion flicker, so its false-alarm share does not cover motion-driven false positives; the motion check complements it.

## 2608-2 FOV 2: a false negative by construction

`aqua_tune/tune_recommendation.txt` (report Oct 14 19:31Z): the common setting for FOV 2 over the three days is thrARScl 3 / minSize 100 with **recall 0.00 on every day and 0.000 events/min** — the one-setting-for-all-days rule found no sensitive setting whose null-movie false-alarm share stayed ≤ 10 % on every day, because on 260922_002 and 261001_002 the null movie produces many on-fibre events at thr ≤ 2 (62 %, 86 % false shares at 1.5/50). The batch at 3/100 gives 0 events in 260915_002 (events csv = header only) → no CFUs. At the most sensitive setting (1.5/50) 260915_002 has 0.51 on-fibre events/min with 0 null events, comparable to FOV 1 (0.64/min) → FOV 2 is not silent; the setting is. Per-day choices would be 1.5/100 (260915), 2.5/100 (260922, still ~nothing), 2/50 (261001: 10.9/min, 9 % false, 41 % off-fibre) — not comparable across days, fine within a day. Why FOV 2's null is "eventful" on two days is open (motion, bleaching or SNR by day) — the per-run motion check on all 24 runs will tell. FOV 1 at the common 2.5/50 is fine (0.6–3 ev/min, ≤ 10 % false). **v34 makes per-day tuning the default for RearChr animals (common box off), which is the route chosen for this.**

## Result (Oct 19, 2026): the comparison ran on samson — AQuA2 alone stays

Runs 260921_004 (pre, 88 % running), 260921_006 (post, 90 %), 260921_002 (pre, 99 %), 260917_006 (sham post, 61 %) × routes A–E, AQuA2 at the run's tuned settings in every route; the legacy wrapper ran in real MATLAB (16–20 min per run with a pool, 0 failed frames). Read from the staged events / CFU / mean-image / shift files (the 1.2 GB `_AQuA2.mat` files stayed on samson, so the motion/residual columns of the html are still empty — `python reg_compare.py report "<mouse>"` on samson fills them; optional). Verdict files on samson: `NavRearChrTIGRE-2608-1\reg_compare\reg_compare_verdict.md` + `.png`.

* **What moves.** Slow drift of the field over a 16-min run: a few px (Python per-bin trace range 3–6 px; AQuA2's integer registration cropped 3–6 px for it; the Oct 14 motion check: median 1–3 px vs the first frames). Within-second jitter of the raw frames: sub-pixel — the legacy DFT stage (sub-pixel, frame vs the mean of its 15-frame chunk) finds median 0.11–0.17 px, p95 0.25–0.42 px, max 0.5–1.8 px. Running vs still: no difference in either estimator (legacy 0.12/0.10, 0.17/0.18, 0.15/0.15, 0.16/0.14 px; Python 1.36/1.57, 0.70/0.50, 0.70/0.78, 0.84/0.61 px). Locomotion adds no XY shift in this animal; z-motion is not measurable this way.
* **A (AQuA2 alone)** gives the sharpest registered mean image of the three whole-pixel routes in every run (Laplacian variance: B 0.88–1.00 of A, C 0.90–0.98). Its events are the robust core: 0–2 per run are found by A only, the rest by the other routes too.
* **B / C (Python pre-registration)**: the per-raw-frame estimates are noisy at raw-frame SNR (correlation to the reference 0.27–0.49; the apparent 1–2 px within-bin range is estimation noise — correcting it sharpens nothing), so the pre-registration adds a little blur and nothing else. The slow component of its trace is right (agrees with AQuA2's crop range).
* **D / E (legacy chain)**: cannot see the slow drift by construction (stage 1 is intra-chunk; stage 2's optimiser moved the frames 0.02–0.05 px median, max 5 px on a few frames with up to 1.3° rotation) — E, AQuA2 after it, still cropped 3–5 px, like A on the raw movie. Its two bilinear resamplings per raw frame (imtranslate + imwarp) smooth the pixel noise: the D/E mean images keep only 0.58–0.70 of A's fine detail although the applied shifts are ~0.15 px (two bilinear shifts of 0.15 px alone give 0.59 — verified numerically on A's mean image). AQuA2's thresholds are in noise-σ units, so at the same tuned settings the smoothed movie yields 2–4× more events (D 32 / 35 / 83 / 115 vs A 7 / 11 / 40 / 35 for 917_006 / 002 / 004 / 006), most found by no other route (23 / 13 / 14 / 66 "only here"), weaker than the events every route finds (peak dF/F 0.17 / 0.35 / 0.22 / 0.23 vs 0.16 / 0.28 / 0.29 / 0.34) and longer; E does not reproduce them (own extras 1 / 12 / 49 / 5). Threshold artefacts of a changed noise floor, not missed events. On-fibre shares are similar across routes (D 0.94 / 0.74 / 0.55 / 0.50 vs A 1.00 / 0.91 / 0.45 / 0.34).
* **Decision: keep the pipeline as it is** — AQuA2's own registration (`registrateCorrect = 2`) on the binned movie, `unpack_bin.py` without `--register`; do not adopt the legacy chain, alone or combined. The v33 motion check stays as the per-run guard (QC report). Nothing of the earlier analyses has to be redone for registration reasons; the v34 rerun (per-day tuning, new recordings, three triggers) proceeds on the existing route.
* Still open and not addressable by 2-D registration: z-motion and the slow 1–2 px non-rigid warp (suite2p nonrigid / NoRMCorre would be the tools; not pursued).
